## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was impressed by its wide range of cloud services, global infrastructure, scalability, and reliability. AWS provides many services that can support different business needs, from computing and storage to databases, networking, and security.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) are all major cloud computing platforms that provide similar services. They offer computing, storage, databases, networking, security, scalability, and application development services. All three also have global infrastructures that allow organizations to deploy applications and services in different geographic locations.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements influence cloud platform selection because organizations need to choose a platform that matches their budget, security requirements, existing technologies, performance needs, scalability, and future goals. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while a business that needs a wide range of cloud services may choose AWS. Organizations focused on data analytics and artificial intelligence may consider GCP.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that selecting a cloud platform requires careful analysis of the client's needs. I learned that it is important to compare cloud platforms based on services, security, scalability, infrastructure, cost, and business requirements. I also learned that the best cloud solution depends on the specific situation and goals of the client.
